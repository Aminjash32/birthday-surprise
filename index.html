You don't have to think twice about telling me or worry about being judged. Just message me and say what's on your mind.

I'll listen, obviously. And if you need an honest opinion or a reality check, haa ji, batayen, we'll talk about that too. 😂 I won't blindly agree with everything you say, but I'll always hear you out properly. I don't know if you consider me your best friend, and there's absolutely no pressure to put a label on anything. But from my side, I do consider you one. I'm genuinely glad we became friends, and I hope we keep making more memories, having random conversations, annoying each other, and being our usual selves.

So yes, consider this a rare display of effort and emotional expression from my side. Please acknowledge accordingly.

Anyway, I hope this year brings you more peace, good memories, plenty of reasons to laugh, good food, and people who make life feel a little easier. Keep being yourself, take care of yourself, and remember that even when things get difficult, you don't have to deal with everything alone.

Happy birthday, Selina. Here's to better days, more laughs, and plenty of lovely little moments ahead. ♡ 💜
